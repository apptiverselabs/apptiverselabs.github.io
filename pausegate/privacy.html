Privacy Policy for PauseGate: Screen Time & Focus

Last Updated: November 25, 2026  |  Effective Date: November 25, 2026

ApptiVerse Labs (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built PauseGate: Screen Time & Focus (“the App”) as a Freemium digital wellbeing and productivity application. This service is provided by ApptiVerse Labs and is designed to help users overcome digital distractions, break unconscious phone habits, and build mindful screen time routines.

We are deeply committed to user privacy. PauseGate operates on a 100% private, on-device architecture. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, screen content, or browsing habits to external servers.

1. Information Collection and Storage

A. Local On-Device Data

All user settings, focus schedules, blocked app selections, restricted websites, blocked keywords, mindful breathing records, streaks, and gamification badges are stored exclusively in your device's internal storage (using Android Jetpack DataStore / SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to ApptiVerse Labs or any third party.

B. Personal Data

We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or files. No user account or registration is required to use PauseGate.

2. Android Permissions and Sensitive APIs

To provide mindful distraction blocking and screen time insights, PauseGate requests specific Android permissions. We believe in complete transparency regarding why these permissions are required:

A. AccessibilityService API (Prominent Disclosure)

PauseGate utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API strictly to provide its core digital wellbeing functionality:

  • Purpose: To detect when the user launches a distracting application, navigates to a restricted website, or searches a blocked keyword that they have intentionally placed on their blocklist during active focus sessions, scheduled routines, or 24/7 mindful protection. Upon detection, PauseGate presents a calming friction overlay to interrupt impulsive habits.
  • No Sensitive Data Collection: PauseGate does NOT log keystrokes, does NOT record screen content, does NOT inspect private messages, and does NOT capture passwords, payment details, or personal information.
  • No Remote Transmission: No information observed by the Accessibility Service is ever recorded, stored remotely, or transmitted across the internet.
Strict Financial & Essential App Exemption:

PauseGate includes a hardcoded safety whitelist. The Accessibility Service strictly and unconditionally excludes Indian and international banking applications (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, etc.), UPI payment platforms (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED), railway booking platforms (IRCTC Rail Connect), emergency dialers, and system settings. These applications are never inspected, monitored, or overlaid.

B. Usage Access Permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

  • Purpose: Allows PauseGate to read foreground application events and calculate daily screen time, app launch counts, and focus session statistics displayed in your local Insights dashboard. It also serves as a secondary fallback to reliably detect blocked apps when Accessibility is inactive.
  • Privacy: All usage statistics are processed 100% locally on your phone and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

C. Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW / Full-Screen Overlay)

  • Purpose: Required to display the serene mindful pause screen and breathing countdown directly over blocked apps when distraction is intercepted.

D. Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

  • Purpose: Used solely to deliver focus countdown notifications, active session status bars, and scheduled routine reminders.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your schedules, blocked lists, focus statistics, and badges are stored locally on your device:

  • You have complete control over your data at all times.
  • You can permanently delete all data generated by PauseGate at any moment by clearing the app's cache and data in your device's Android Settings > Apps > PauseGate > Storage > Clear Data, or simply by uninstalling the application.

5. Security

We value your trust in choosing PauseGate to regain control over your screen time. Because our architecture is strictly offline and does not transmit your personal or usage data to external servers, your activity remains completely private to you on your personal device.

6. Children’s Privacy

PauseGate is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to third-party services through our App, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Privacy Policy for Digital Japa Mala

Last updated: August 2026

ApptiVerse Labs built the Digital Japa Mala app as a Freemium ad-supported application. This SERVICE is provided by ApptiVerse Labs at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Privacy First: All your mantra chanting counts, daily history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not host user accounts or store your spiritual logs on external servers.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, your daily counts and preferences remain on your device. The app does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for service delivery and advertising:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising and analytics)

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback with each bead count and upon completing a full 108-bead mala.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Used optionally to keep the display active during prayer sessions if selected in settings.

3. Log Data

In case of an error or crash in the app, we may collect data and information through Google Play Console (Crashlytics/ANR reports) on your phone called Log Data. This data may include information such as your device model, operating system version, and system state at the time of the error.

4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting to this page.

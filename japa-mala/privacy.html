Privacy Policy for Digital Japa Mala

Last Updated: November 2026

ApptiVerse Labs ("we," "our," or "us") built the Digital Japa Mala app as an ad-supported Freemium application. This SERVICE is provided by ApptiVerse Labs at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and your privacy rights when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Local Data Privacy

We believe your spiritual chanting practice should remain private, peaceful, and sacred.

  • No User Accounts or Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts) to use the app.
  • 100% Local Storage: All your daily counts, completed rounds, custom mantras, counter profiles, wallpaper preferences, achievement badges, and history logs are stored strictly on your device's internal storage (SharedPreferences and local app database). We do not collect, store, or transmit your chanting data or spiritual logs to any external server.

2. Third-Party Services and Automatically Collected Data

To provide advertisements and process optional in-app purchases, the app integrates trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety standards, these services collect and process certain technical data:

A. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and exit rewarded video ads. To deliver relevant ads and detect fraud, Google AdMob automatically collects:

  • Approximate Location: General location derived from your device's IP address (not precise GPS location).
  • Device or Other Identifiers: Your Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app set identifiers used for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, and contextual/personalized advertising.
  • App Activity & Interactions: Ad impressions, views, clicks, and interaction telemetry.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Crash logs, app launch times, and performance metrics to ensure SDK stability.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

B. Google Play In-App Billing

If you purchase the optional "Remove Ads" feature, transaction processing is handled securely and entirely by Google Play Store Billing. We never receive or store your credit card, bank, or payment details.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests only standard Android permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required for loading Google AdMob advertisements and verifying Google Play Billing status.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver scheduled daily chanting reminders that you can turn on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide haptic feedback when tapping the chant counter.
  • Photos / Media Storage (Photo Picker): Used only if you choose to set a custom deity photo as your background wallpaper from your phone's gallery. Your photos remain on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

4. Data Storage, Backup, and PDF Export

  • Local Backups: If you use the "Backup Data" feature, your backup text file is saved locally to your device or shared via your device's native share menu (e.g., to your personal Google Drive). We have no access to your Google Drive account or files.
  • PDF Activity Reports: Exported PDF history reports are generated entirely on your device and saved directly to your device's Downloads folder.

5. Security & Encryption in Transit

  • All technical data transmitted by third-party SDKs (AdMob and Google Play Billing) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Because all chanting data is kept locally on your phone, your personal counts are not exposed to internet vulnerabilities.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & Opt-Out

  • Deleting Your Practice Data: You can delete your counter profiles and history at any time using the in-app Reset/Delete buttons, or by clearing the app's data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Digital Japa Mala > Clear Data), or by uninstalling the application.
  • Managing Advertising ID: You can control, reset, or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time by navigating to your device's Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app enhancements or policy updates. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any modifications are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.

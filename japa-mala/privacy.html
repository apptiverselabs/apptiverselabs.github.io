Information Collection & Local Privacy

ApptiVerse Labs built the Digital Japa Mala app as an ad-supported Freemium application. This Service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, transmit, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, or home address.
  • Local Data Storage: All your custom counter profiles, bead settings, target rounds, completed malas, daily streaks, wallpaper preferences, and aggregated history logs are stored strictly on your phone's internal storage (`SharedPreferences` and local app storage).
2

Third-Party SDKs & Data Safety Declaration

To support free development through advertisements and allow voluntary ad-free upgrades, the application incorporates verified third-party libraries provided by Google. In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, these services collect the following telemetry:

📢 Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

Used to deliver banner, interstitial, and exit video ads. Google AdMob automatically collects:

Approximate Location (IP Address) Device / Advertising ID (AAID) App Interactions & Ad Views Crash Logs & Diagnostics

This technical data is used for fraud prevention, ad frequency capping, and contextual/personalized ad delivery. For detailed privacy practices, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • How Google Uses Information From Partner Sites/Apps ↗
💳 Google Play In-App Billing

If you purchase the optional "Remove Ads" feature, your payment is processed directly and securely by Google Play Store Billing. ApptiVerse Labs never receives, sees, or stores your banking or credit card details.

3

Device Permissions & Transparent Usage

Digital Japa Mala requests only standard system permissions strictly required for its features:

🌐 Network State INTERNET

Loads AdMob advertisements and verifies your Google Play in-app purchase status.

🔔 Daily Reminders POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Sends opt-in daily practice reminders that can be toggled on/off in Settings anytime.

📳 Haptic Touch VIBRATE

Triggers gentle vibration feedback when tapping beads to count without looking at the screen.

🖼️ Photo Gallery PHOTO_PICKER

Allows setting a sacred deity wallpaper from your gallery. Photos stay on-device and are never uploaded.

4

Data Storage, Backup & PDF Export

  • Local Backups: When you generate a backup, your backup text file is saved locally to your device or shared via Android's native share sheet (e.g. to your personal Google Drive). We never have access to your Drive files or storage.
  • PDF Activity Reports: Downloadable activity reports are compiled entirely on-device and saved directly to your local `Downloads` folder.
5

Security & Encryption in Transit

We take safeguarding your information seriously:

  • All technical communication between the app and Google services (AdMob and Google Play) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Because chanting numbers and custom mantra profiles are not uploaded to any remote server, your practice data is isolated from cloud breaches.
6

Data Retention, Deletion & Opt-Out

You have complete control over all data related to the application:

  • Deleting Practice Data: You can wipe all counts at any time using the in-app Reset/Delete buttons, by clearing storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Digital Japa Mala > Clear Data), or by uninstalling the application.
  • Advertising ID Opt-Out: You can reset or permanently delete your device's Android Advertising ID anytime by going to:
    Android Device Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
7

Children's Privacy: Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

Policy Changes: We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates are effective immediately upon being published on this page.
